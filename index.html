About Me

I am Dilasha Lakshmi, a young artist passionate about watercolor, acrylic, sketching and creative illustration. My artwork ranges from landscapes and still life compositions to vibrant portraits, anime-inspired creations and decorative patterns. Over the years, my paintings have received appreciation and recognition at various events and competitions. Beyond art, I enjoy family road trips, exploring new places, cars, good food and staying connected through technology.
